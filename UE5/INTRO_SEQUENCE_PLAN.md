## Procedural environment and materials

The editor script builds the corridor from engine mesh primitives and creates editable material assets under `/Game/Materials`. Environment detailing includes:

- concrete floor, ceiling, walls and far bulkhead;
- repeated structural frames, recessed wall panels, panel slots, access-door frames and hazard bands;
- overhead cable trays, hanging cable drops and three parallel industrial pipes;
- floor drainage channels with repeated grates and inset floor plates;
- six machine/generator housings with vent details and status lamps;
- overhead light housings, safety posts and an illuminated terminal with a NO SIGNAL readout;
- suspended dust-mote geometry;
- concrete, painted-steel, brushed-metal, oxidized-metal, warning-yellow, cyan-emissive, red-alarm and cold-emitter materials.

## Cinematic systems

- 27-second Level Sequence at 24 fps.
- CineCamera with a forward dolly and a camera-cut track.
- Animated studio credit, main title, subtitle and production credit.
- Brief red/cyan interference text echoes.
- Opening and ending fade keys.
- Cold overhead lighting, red warning lights, height fog and post-process contrast/bloom/vignette/chromatic aberration where supported.
- Original stereo WAV generated by the script, with low drone, machine harmonics, radio static, reverse swell and metallic impact.
